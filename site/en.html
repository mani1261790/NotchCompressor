Drag to the notch.

Move the sample video up to reveal three drop zones. Left compresses video, right compresses audio, and center compresses both. Clicking works too.

Leave it in the queue.

Add several videos and drag waiting tasks to change their order. Running tasks stay at the top. Pause and resume each task individually.

Replace only when smaller.

The original is replaced only after the entire output passes validation and is smaller. Failure or no reduction keeps the original.

Features

Add videos together

Drop MOV, MP4, and M4V videos together. Remove waiting or paused tasks individually when you no longer need them.

Compression tools included

No extra tools to install. Mac hardware encodes video as HEVC; audio is encoded as AAC.

Keep working in the background

Close the queue and processing continues. Enable notifications for size savings and failures.

Japanese and English

Follows the macOS per-app language setting. Both installer languages contain the same app.

Quality and size

Choose Higher quality, Balanced, or Smaller files. Custom lets you set the video bitrate percentage and audio bitrate. Re-encoding is lossy; keep a separate copy of originals you need.

Where does the compressed video go?

The app replaces the original at the same location and name only when the entire output passes validation and is smaller. Failure, cancellation, or no size reduction keeps the original. Lost quality cannot be recovered after replacement, so keep a separate copy of any original you need.

Which files are supported?

Local MOV, MP4, and M4V videos. Video re-encoding supports opaque, 8-bit SDR; audio re-encoding supports mono and stereo. Images, standalone audio files, and PDFs are not supported yet.

How do I install and update?

Open the DMG, drag the app to Applications, and launch it from that folder. The app and DMG are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. Check for updates inside the app. Install manually if updating from alpha.1. If alpha.2 stays open during an update, close Settings and quit from the app menu to finish the update.

Does it keep running during sleep?

Active tasks prevent idle sleep without preventing display sleep. Manual system sleep pauses processing until the Mac wakes. Tasks you paused yourself stay paused. After quitting the app or restarting the Mac, unfinished tasks must restart from the beginning.

Is the app available in English?

Yes. The app supports Japanese and English and follows the macOS per-app language setting. Restart the app after changing the language. The Japanese and English DMGs have different installer instructions but contain the same app.

Quality and size savings depend on the video. Review the supported formats and saving behavior before use.
