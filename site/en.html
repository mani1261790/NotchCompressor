Drag to the notch.

Drag the sample video to the notch. Left compresses video, center compresses both video and audio, and right compresses audio. You can also tap the file and choose a zone.

Features

Add videos together

Drop MOV, MP4, and M4V files together to compress multiple videos in a queue.

Adjust quality and size

Choose Higher quality, Balanced, or Smaller files. Fine-tune video and audio bitrate settings independently.

Keep working in the background

Compression continues when you close the queue and resumes after your Mac wakes from sleep. Enable notifications for size savings and failures.

Replace only when smaller

The original is replaced only after the output passes validation and is smaller. Failure or no reduction keeps the original. Keep a separate copy of originals you need.

Your queue. Your pace.

Drag a filename or its handle to change the waiting order. Running tasks stay at the top.

  • Pause and resume individual tasks, keeping their progress.
  • Remove waiting or paused tasks individually. Originals stay intact.
  • Completed tasks leave the queue automatically, keeping the remaining work in view.
