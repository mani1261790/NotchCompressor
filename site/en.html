NotchCompressor

Smaller recordings, straight from the notch.
Drag videos to the top of your screen and choose what to compress.

An early look at the upcoming release. The features and demo below describe 0.1.0, which is still in development. The available alpha.2 download is a Japanese-language preview with some differences in behavior.

Apple Silicon · macOS 14 or later

Source code ↗

Drag to the notch.

Drag the sample video to the notch. Left compresses video, center compresses both video and audio, and right compresses audio. You can also tap the file and choose a zone.

Features

Add videos together

Drop local MOV, MP4, and M4V videos together. Video supports opaque, 8-bit SDR; audio supports mono and stereo. Images, standalone audio, and PDFs are not supported yet.

Compression tools included

No extra tools to install. Mac hardware encodes video as HEVC; audio is encoded as AAC.

Keep working in the background

Processing continues after closing the queue. Enable notifications for size savings and failures. Active tasks prevent idle sleep while allowing the display to turn off.

Japanese and English

Follows the macOS per-app language setting after restarting the app. Japanese and English DMGs differ only in installer instructions; both contain the same app.

Your queue. Your pace.

Drag a filename or its handle to change the waiting order. Running tasks stay at the top.

  • Pause and resume individual tasks, keeping their progress.
  • Remove waiting or paused tasks individually. Originals stay intact.
  • Completed tasks leave the queue automatically, keeping the remaining work in view.

Choose quality and size first.

Choose Higher quality, Balanced, or Smaller files. Custom lets you set the video bitrate percentage and audio bitrate. Results depend on the video.

Replace only when smaller.

The original is replaced at the same name and location only after the entire output passes validation and is smaller. Failure, cancellation, or no reduction keeps the original. Re-encoding is lossy, so keep a separate copy of originals you need.

Sleep. Wake. Continue.

Manual sleep pauses processing until the Mac wakes. Tasks you paused yourself stay paused. After quitting the app or restarting the Mac, unfinished tasks restart from the beginning.

Install it. Get started.

Open the DMG, drag the app to Applications, and launch it from that folder. The app and DMG are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. Check for updates inside the app.

Install manually when updating from alpha.1. If alpha.2 stays open during an update, close Settings and quit from the app menu to finish the update.
