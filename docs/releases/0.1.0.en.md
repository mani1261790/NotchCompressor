# NotchCompressor 0.1.0

The first stable release of the macOS app for compressing screen recordings at the notch.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon. Download the `-en.dmg` installer and drag the app to Applications. Japanese and English DMGs contain the same bilingual app. The app and DMGs are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple.

- Add multiple videos, reorder waiting tasks, and pause, resume or remove individual tasks.
- Redesigned queue and settings, with Japanese and English interfaces and notifications.
- Replace originals only when output is smaller and passes full decoding validation. Failed, cancelled or non-shrinking output leaves originals intact.
- Resume after system sleep while preserving manual pauses.
- Preserve source frame timing or choose up to 30 fps. Existing settings and queued tasks retain their settings.
- Sparkle updates. Hardware HEVC remains the default.

Replacement removes the uncompressed original. Keep a separate copy when needed. Supported inputs are MOV, MP4 and M4V videos.

Alpha.1 requires manual installation. If alpha.2 does not quit after Install and Relaunch, close Settings and quit the app from its menu. This is fixed in 0.1.0.

Build 11, source `d5a8753`. Signing, notarization, CI and distribution checks passed. Login launch after an actual relogin, visible notification banners, mouse-drop completion in the redesigned queue and additional-display behavior still have unverified coverage. Quality across representative real recordings remains under evaluation.
