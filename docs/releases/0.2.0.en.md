# NotchCompressor 0.2.0

Compress images and audio, and bring a clock and Apple Music to your notch.

- Added JPEG, PNG, and AAC M4A compression. Add them together with videos; drop targets adapt to the files.
- Set JPEG quality and standalone audio bitrate independently. PNG optimization preserves pixels and transparency.
- Hover over the notch to see the clock, song title, and album artwork, with play/pause control. Choose Off, On hover, or Always independently for clock and music.
- Simplified Settings with explanations behind an info button and Liquid Glass selection controls on supported macOS versions.
- Improved opening and closing transitions to match the width of persistent widgets.
- Clearer guidance when notification permission is missing. Automatic update checks are enabled for new installations; existing preferences are preserved.

## Install

Requires macOS 14 or later and Apple Silicon. Open the `-en.dmg` or `-ja.dmg` and drag the app to Applications. The installer instructions differ, but both contain the same app. The app and DMGs are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. Version 0.1.0 can also check for updates in the app.

## Formats and originals

Supports MOV/MP4/M4V video, JPEG/PNG images, and AAC M4A audio. Images are limited to 128 MB and 32 megapixels. APNG, HEIF, HDR images, ALAC, WAV, and MP3 are not supported in this release. M4A requires one mono or stereo AAC track; unsupported metadata or layouts keep the original. Music integration supports Apple Music.

**Only smaller output that passes validation replaces the original at the same location. The previous file is not retained after replacement; keep a separate copy when needed.** Failure, cancellation, or no size reduction preserves the original.
